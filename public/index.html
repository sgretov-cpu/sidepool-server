Prototype, not a wagering product. Every credit is virtual, free to top up, and worth nothing outside this page. It demonstrates the round → split-pool → settlement mechanic behind a "Versus" style feature: everyone here shares the same live rounds — two sides collect stakes, one side wins, and the pool splits among winners in proportion to their stake, no fixed odds.

Connect your wallet

Your wallet address is your account here — no email, no password, nothing to sign up for. Connecting asks your wallet to sign one free message proving you own the address; it requests no transaction and moves no funds. Everything you play with stays virtual.
